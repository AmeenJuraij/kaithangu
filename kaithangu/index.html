03 · DOCUMENTS

Document checklist

Generic preparation checklist. Exact requirements vary by scheme and must be verified.

Identity document
Keep an accepted identity document available.
Address / residence proof
Verify what the responsible office accepts.
Bank account details
Where the scheme requires payment through a bank account.
Registration / membership proof
Important for schemes tied to welfare-board membership.
Income / category certificate
Only where the selected scheme requires it.
Scheme-specific evidence
Examples can include worker, fisher, student or asset-related records.
04 · NEXT STEP

Where and how to apply

🏢 Responsible department / welfare board

For fisheries welfare schemes, the Kerala Fisheries Department identifies district offices and the Kerala Fishermen Welfare Fund Board / Matsyafed for relevant schemes.

Official Fisheries scheme page ↗

🧾 Akshaya

Akshaya centres provide multiple citizen and e-District services. Use an Akshaya centre when the relevant application or certificate service is available there.

Akshaya citizen services ↗

🏛 Labour welfare

For labour-welfare schemes, verify the current process with the Kerala Labour Commissionerate or the responsible welfare board.

Labour Commissionerate ↗
